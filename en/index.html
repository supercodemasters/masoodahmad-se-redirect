Services I sell

Every assignment starts from how you work today, not from a ready-made product.

  • Custom systems

    Web systems and internal tools that follow your way of working, instead of spreadsheets and manual steps.

  • ERP and finance

    Purchasing, inventory, orders and bookkeeping records gathered in one system.

  • Integrations

    Your programs are connected so data moves automatically and is entered once.

  • Dashboards and reports

    Key figures from several sources in one place, updated without manual work.

  • Customer and staff portals

    Logged-in pages where customers or employees find and handle what they need themselves.

  • AI apps and automation

    AI that sorts, summarises and answers where it saves time, with a person still making the decision.

  • Visibility on Google

    Technical review, faster pages and clear structure so the right customers find you.

How we work

  1. Call

    A short video or phone call about what takes unnecessary time.

  2. Proposal

    A written proposal with scope, timeline and price.

  3. Delivery

    The build happens in small steps that you test along the way.

  4. Support

    Fixes and further development after go-live.

Frequently asked questions

Which businesses do you work with?

Businesses of all sizes in Örebro and the rest of Sweden that want less manual work or a better overview.

What does an assignment cost?

It depends on the scope. After the first call you get a written proposal with a fixed or hourly price.

How do we start?

With a short video or phone call that you book in the calendar. It is free of charge.

Can you work with our existing programs?

Yes. Existing programs are often connected rather than replaced.

Which languages do you work in?

Swedish and English, in calls, documentation and in what is built.
